About Kyrie Irving

Kyrie Irving is an esteemed NBA player with a decorated professional basketball career. Beyond having some of the best handles in the NBA, he also has great insights on self fulfillment and understanding that often times your worst enemy is your own mind. Because of this, many find him to be inspirational, not only through his basketball talent and skill, but because of his understanding of the struggles faced by many people in many situations.
